## I. The Agony in the Garden
1. Who went with His disciples after supper to the Mount of Olives. (Lk 22:39)
2. Who took Peter, James, and John with Him to watch and pray. (Mt 26:36)
3. Who fell with His face to the ground in deep anguish. (Mt 26:39)
4. Who prayed: "My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from me; yet not as I will, but as you will." (Mt 26:39)
5. Whose sweat became like great drops of blood falling to the ground. (Lk 22:44)
6. Who returned to the disciples and found them sleeping. (Mt 26:40)
7. Who asked: "Could you not watch with me one hour?" (Mt 26:40)
8. Who prayed a second time: "My Father, if this cannot pass unless I drink it, your will be done." (Mt 26:42)
9. Who came again and found them asleep, for their eyes were heavy. (Mt 26:43)
10. Who prayed a third time, saying the same words. (Mt 26:44)

## II. The Scourging at the Pillar
1. Whom they bound and led away to Pontius Pilate. (Mk 15:1)
2. Whom Pilate asked: "Are you the King of the Jews?" (Mk 15:2)
3. Who replied: "My kingdom is not of this world." (Jn 18:36)
4. Of whom Pilate told the Jews: "I find no basis for a charge against him." (Jn 18:38)
5. Whom Pilate then took and had scourged. (Jn 19:1)
6. Who had no beauty or majesty to attract us, a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief. (Isa 53:2–3)
7. Who bore our griefs and carried our sorrows. (Isa 53:4)
8. Who was pierced for our transgressions, crushed for our iniquities; by His wounds we are healed. (Isa 53:5)
9. Who was oppressed and afflicted, yet He opened not His mouth. (Isa 53:7)
10. Who was led like a lamb to the slaughter, silent before its shearers. (Isa 53:7)

## III. The Crowning with Thorns
1. Whom the soldiers stripped and put on a scarlet robe. (Mt 27:28)
2. For whom they twisted together a crown of thorns and set it on His head. (Mt 27:29)
3. Into whose right hand they put a staff as a mock scepter. (Mt 27:29)
4. Whom they mocked, spat upon, and struck repeatedly on the head. (Mt 27:29–30)
5. Of whom Pilate declared: "I am innocent of this man's blood; see to it yourselves." (Mt 27:24)
6. Who came out wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe. (Jn 19:5)
7. Of whom Pilate said: "Behold the man!" (Jn 19:5)
8. Against whom the crowd shouted: "Crucify him! Crucify him!" (Jn 19:6)
9. Instead of whom they demanded the release of Barabbas. (Lk 23:18)
10. Who was handed over to their will to be crucified. (Mt 27:26)

## IV. The Carrying of the Cross
1. Jesus is condemned to death: "Do not judge, and you will not be judged." (Lk 6:37)
2. Jesus takes up His cross: "Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart." (Mt 11:29)
3. Jesus falls the first time: "Whoever wants to be my disciple must deny themselves and take up their cross and follow me." (Mk 8:34)
4. Jesus meets His afflicted Mother: "And a sword will pierce your own soul too." (Lk 2:35)
5. Simon of Cyrene helps Jesus carry His cross: "Carry each other’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." (Gal 6:2)
6. Veronica wipes the face of Jesus: "Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me." (Mt 25:40)
7. Jesus falls the second time: "If you think you are standing firm, be careful that you don’t fall!" (1 Cor 10:12)
8. Jesus comforts the women of Jerusalem: "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me; weep for yourselves and for your children." (Lk 23:28)
9. Jesus falls the third time: "For if people do these things when the tree is green, what will happen when it is dry?" (Lk 23:31)
10. Jesus is stripped of His garments: "Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one's life for one's friends." (Jn 15:13)

## V. The Crucifixion and Death
1. Whom they brought to Golgotha, the Place of the Skull, and crucified between two criminals. (Lk 23:33)
2. Who said: "Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing." (Lk 23:34)
3. Who said: "I thirst." (Jn 19:28)
4. To the repentant thief who asked to be remembered, He answered: "Truly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise." (Lk 23:42–43)
5. Who said to His Mother: "Woman, behold your son," and to the beloved disciple: "Behold your mother." (Jn 19:26–27)
6. Who cried out with a loud voice: "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (Mt 27:46)
7. Who proclaimed: "It is finished!" (Jn 19:30)
8. Who prayed: "Father, into your hands I commit my spirit." (Lk 23:46)
9. Whose body was taken down and laid gently in the arms of His Mother.
10. Who laid down His life for us in supreme love. (1 Jn 3:16)
