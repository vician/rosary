---
title: Glorious Mysteries
---

_Source: Salesian Cooperators — Formation materials._

## I. The Resurrection
1. Who rose on the third day in accordance with the Scriptures. (1 Cor 15:4)
2. Who appeared after His resurrection to the holy women. (Mt 28:9)
3. Who appeared to the disciples on the shore and in the upper room. (Mt 28:17)
4. Who showed His wounded hands and side to Thomas. (Jn 20:26)
5. Who said: "Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed." (Jn 20:29)
6. Over whom death no longer has any mastery. (Rom 6:9)
7. Who said: "I am the resurrection and the life." (Jn 11:25)
8. Who said: "The one who believes in me will live, even though they die." (Jn 11:25)
9. Who was raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep. (1 Cor 15:20)
10. With whom we also shall appear with Him in glory. (Col 3:4)

## II. The Ascension
1. Who appeared to the disciples for forty days and spoke of the Kingdom of God. (Acts 1:3)
2. Who lifted up His hands and blessed them as He parted from them. (Lk 24:51)
3. Who was taken up before their very eyes, and a cloud hid Him from their sight. (Acts 1:9)
4. Who was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God. (Acts 1:11)
5. Who is at the right hand of God and is interceding for us. (Rom 8:34)
6. Who will come back in the same way you have seen Him go into heaven. (Acts 1:11)
7. Who said: "I am going there to prepare a place for you." (Jn 14:2)
8. Who said: "My Father’s house has many rooms." (Jn 14:2)
9. Who said: "And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age." (Mt 28:20)
10. Who is the same yesterday and today and forever. (Heb 13:8)

## III. The Descent of the Holy Spirit
1. Who was anointed with the Holy Spirit and with power. (Acts 10:38)
2. Who promised the Advocate, the Spirit of truth, who goes out from the Father. (Jn 15:26)
3. Who said: "When he, the Spirit of truth, comes, he will guide you into all the truth." (Jn 16:13)
4. Whose Holy Spirit filled the Apostles with tongues as of fire. (Acts 2:4)
5. Who poured out the Holy Spirit in abundant measure. (Acts 2:33)
6. By whose Spirit the Apostles were led to the ends of the earth. (Acts 16:7)
7. Through whom the Apostles imparted the Holy Spirit by the laying on of hands. (Acts 8:17)
8. Who gave us the Spirit as a deposit and guarantee. (2 Cor 5:5)
9. Whose Spirit calls out within our hearts: "Abba, Father!" (Gal 4:6)
10. In whose name the Father sent the Holy Spirit to teach us all things. (Jn 14:26)

## IV. The Assumption of Mary
1. Who chose you from all eternity to be His Mother. (cf. Gen 3:15)
2. Who preserved you from original sin through the merits of His redemption.
3. Who was born of you in the fullness of time. (Gal 4:4)
4. Who granted your petition at the wedding feast in Cana. (Jn 2:3–5)
5. On whose account Elizabeth proclaimed you blessed among women. (Lk 1:42)
6. Who entrusted His beloved disciple to your motherly care. (Jn 19:26)
7. Who gave you to all humanity as our Mother. (Jn 19:27)
8. Who did not allow your holy body to undergo corruption.
9. Who assumed you, body and soul, into heavenly glory.
10. With whom you shine forever in celestial beauty.

## V. The Coronation of Mary
1. Who crowned you Queen of heaven and earth.
2. With whom you reign in everlasting joy.
3. Before whom you intercede for us with maternal love.
4. Who entrusted us to your protection and guidance.
5. Who gave you to the Church as the model of faith and unceasing prayer.
6. Who adorned you with all heavenly virtues.
7. Who will return in glory at the end of time.
8. Who will judge the living and the dead.
9. In whom all things in heaven and on earth are restored.
10. For whose glorious coming we eagerly wait.
