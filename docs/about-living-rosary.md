## What is it?
The Living Rosary is a prayer community of believers who, in addition to their regular prayers, commit to praying one decade of the Rosary each day. The decades are distributed among the members of the group in such a way that together, the group completes the entire Rosary every day.

## What is its purpose?
Its primary purpose is the power of shared, unified prayer. When five people join together, they pray a full rosary each day. Praying one decade is easy and accessible for everyone. Especially during significant, joyful, or difficult events in life, it is immensely uplifting and strengthening when all members unite with one intention, bringing it before God. It also helps each participant build faithfulness and perseverance in prayer.

## What intentions do we pray for?
God's embrace is vast enough to hold everything we bring to Him in prayer. In addition to our own personal intentions, we include a shared intention for the entire rosary group.

In each group of 5 members, the assigned decade rotates every week. The member praying the first decade of the rosary that week sets the collective prayer intention for the week. This allows members to share what is on their hearts and support one another in prayer.

## How to pray a decade of the Living Rosary

- **Structure of a Decade:**
	- The Lord's Prayer (*Our Father*)
	- 10× *Hail Mary*
	- *Glory Be to the Father...*
	- Fatima Prayer (*O My Jesus, forgive us our sins...*)
- **Joyful Mysteries:**
	- The Annunciation
	- The Visitation
	- The Nativity
	- The Presentation in the Temple
	- The Finding in the Temple
- **Luminous Mysteries:**
	- The Baptism of Jesus in the Jordan
	- The Wedding at Cana
	- The Proclamation of the Kingdom
	- The Transfiguration
	- The Institution of the Eucharist
- **Sorrowful Mysteries:**
	- The Agony in the Garden
	- The Scourging at the Pillar
	- The Crowning with Thorns
	- The Carrying of the Cross
	- The Crucifixion and Death of Jesus
- **Glorious Mysteries:**
	- The Resurrection
	- The Ascension
	- The Descent of the Holy Spirit
	- The Assumption of Mary
	- The Coronation of Mary
