## Living Rosary

The Living Rosary is a prayer community of believers who, in addition to their daily prayers, commit to praying one decade of the Rosary each day. This website helps organize such groups so that together the members pray the entire Rosary each day.

## How to use

On the [custom group](group.md) page, you can generate a shared link for your 5-member prayer group and distribute it among your members.

If you commit to praying all 3 (or 4) rosaries a week, or a rosary every day, you can use our [Weekly Rosary Tracker](tracker.md) to track your prayer progress and individual Hail Marys.
