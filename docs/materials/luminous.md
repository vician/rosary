---
title: Luminous Mysteries
---

_Source: Salesian Cooperators — Formation materials._

## I. The Baptism of Jesus in the Jordan
1. Whose public ministry was preceded by years of hidden life.
2. Who grew in wisdom and grace in the home of Mary and Joseph.
3. Who was constantly in communion and prayer with His Father.
4. Who humbly bore with human weakness and imperfections.
5. Who manifested obedience to the Father in ordinary daily acts.
6. Who patiently waited until His hour had come.
7. Who was anointed by the Holy Spirit for His messianic mission.
8. Who surrendered entirely to the Father's will.
9. Who proclaimed the Good News to all.
10. Who returned from the Jordan full of the Holy Spirit.

## II. The Wedding at Cana
1. Who was invited with Mary and His disciples to a wedding feast.
2. In whom you recognized that the appointed hour had arrived.
3. To whom you said: "They have no wine."
4. Who commanded the jars to be filled to the brim with water.
5. Who performed His first sign through your intercession.
6. Whose divine glory remained hidden from many.
7. Who invites our readiness to do whatever He tells us.
8. Who speaks to us to open our hearts.
9. Who inspires us in how we ought to live and act.
10. Who transforms our lives into something abundant and new.

## III. The Proclamation of the Kingdom
1. Long awaited by the people of God.
2. Who came to free humanity from the slavery of sin.
3. Who became the true hope of eternal life.
4. Who brings the joyful message of the Gospel.
5. Who liberates us from oppressive bonds and sinful inclinations.
6. Who calls us not only to hear the Gospel, but to repentance and conversion.
7. Whose presence transforms the human heart.
8. Who gives Himself completely and asks for our open hearts in return.
9. Whose love sets our souls on fire with true joy.
10. With whose Gospel we gladly take up our daily cross.

## IV. The Transfiguration
1. Who revealed His glory and majesty to the disciples before His Passion.
2. Who manifested His divine sovereignty with the Father.
3. Who placed in the hearts of the Apostles the certainty of His victory over death.
4. Who revealed the mystery of His greatness on Mount Tabor.
5. Who displayed the beauty of His glorified body.
6. Who strengthened the faith of the Apostles for the hour of the Cross.
7. Who wished this vision to remain secret until His resurrection.
8. Who calls each of us to intimate encounter and prayer with Him.
9. Whose radiant majesty filled the Apostles with holy awe.
10. In whose presence we find divine peace and protection.

## V. The Institution of the Eucharist
1. Who, though eternal and almighty, placed Himself into human hands.
2. Who chose to remain close to us in sacrament rather than depart.
3. Who comes to us in the Eucharist in quiet humility.
4. Who comes as bread in gentle stillness.
5. Who came not to be served, but to serve.
6. Who becomes our true spiritual food.
7. Who feeds our hungry souls and satisfies our deepest longings.
8. Who preserves our spiritual life through Holy Communion unto eternity.
9. Whose gift of love surpasses all human understanding.
10. Who heals our bodies and souls in the Most Blessed Sacrament.
