title: Rosary for Priestly Vocations
---

_Source: [Archdiocesan Seminary Prague](http://www.arcs.cuni.cz/) — [Meditative Rosary for Priestly Vocations](http://www.arcs.cuni.cz/index.php?id=5&id2=16)._

## Decade I
1. Jesus, who said to Nathanael: "I saw you while you were still under the fig tree before Philip called you." (Jn 1:48)
2. Jesus, who said to Philip: "Follow me." (Jn 1:43)
3. Jesus, who said to Simon and Andrew: "Come, follow me, and I will send you out to fish for people," and at once they left their nets and followed Him. (cf. Mk 1:17–18)
4. Jesus, who saw James son of Zebedee and his brother John mending their nets, and immediately called them; they left their father Zebedee and followed Him. (cf. Mk 1:19–20)
5. Jesus, who saw Matthew sitting at the tax collector's booth and said: "Follow me," and he got up and followed Him. (cf. Mt 9:9)
6. Jesus, who went up on a mountainside and called to Him those He wanted, and they came to Him. (Mk 3:13)
7. Jesus, who sent out the Apostles to preach the good news and heal everywhere. (cf. Lk 9:6)
8. Jesus, who said: "For I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners." (Mt 9:13)
9. Jesus, who looked at the rich young man and loved him, saying: "Go, sell everything you have and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me." (Mk 10:21)
10. Jesus, who said: "No one can come to me unless the Father has enabled them." (Jn 6:65)

## Decade II
1. Jesus, who prayed to the Father: "As you sent me into the world, I have sent them into the world." (Jn 17:18)
2. Jesus, who said: "I sent you to reap what you have not worked for. Others have done the hard work, and you have reaped the benefits of their labor." (Jn 4:38)
3. Jesus, who said: "If you hold to my teaching, you are really my disciples. Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free." (Jn 8:31–32)
4. Jesus, who said: "As long as it is day, we must do the works of him who sent me. Night is coming, when no one can work." (Jn 9:4)
5. Jesus, who said: "Very truly I tell you, no servant is greater than his master, nor is a messenger greater than the one who sent him." (Jn 13:16)
6. Jesus, who said: "I no longer call you servants, because a servant does not know his master's business." (Jn 15:15a)
7. Jesus, who said: "Instead, I have called you friends, for everything that I learned from my Father I have made known to you." (Jn 15:15b)
8. Jesus, who said: "You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you so that you might go and bear fruit—fruit that will last." (Jn 15:16a)
9. Jesus, who said to the Apostles: "Peace be with you! As the Father has sent me, I am sending you." (Jn 20:21)
10. Jesus, who breathed on them and said: "Receive the Holy Spirit. If you forgive anyone’s sins, their sins are forgiven; if you do not forgive them, they are not forgiven." (Jn 20:22b–23)

## Decade III
1. Jesus, who appointed twelve that they might be with Him and that He might send them out to preach. (cf. Mk 3:14–15)
2. Jesus, who said: "I am sending you out like sheep among wolves. Therefore be as shrewd as snakes and as innocent as doves." (Mt 10:16)
3. Jesus, who said: "What I tell you in the dark, speak in the daylight; what is whispered in your ear, proclaim from the roofs." (Mt 10:27)
4. Jesus, who said: "When you enter a house, first say: Peace to this house." (Lk 10:5)
5. Jesus, who said: "Heal the sick who are there and tell them: The kingdom of God has come near to you." (Lk 10:9)
6. Jesus, who said: "I have given you authority to trample on snakes and scorpions and to overcome all the power of the enemy." (Lk 10:19)
7. Jesus, who said to Simon Peter: "Feed my lambs... Take care of my sheep." (Jn 21:15c–16)
8. Jesus, who said: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." (Mt 28:18–19)
9. Jesus, who said: "Teach them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age." (Mt 28:20)
10. Jesus, who said: "No one who puts a hand to the plow and looks back is fit for service in the kingdom of God." (Lk 9:62)

## Decade IV
1. Jesus, who said: "The secret of the kingdom of God has been given to you." (Mk 4:11)
2. Jesus, who said: "Very truly I tell you, whoever believes in me will do the works I have been doing, and they will do even greater things than these, because I am going to the Father." (Jn 14:12)
3. Jesus, who said: "And I will do whatever you ask in my name, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son." (Jn 14:13)
4. Jesus, who said: "The world cannot accept the Spirit of truth, because it neither sees him nor knows him. But you know him, for he lives with you and will be in you." (Jn 14:17)
5. Jesus, who said: "Remain in me, as I also remain in you." (Jn 15:4a)
6. Jesus, who said: "I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing." (Jn 15:5)
7. Jesus, who said: "This is to my Father's glory, that you bear much fruit, showing yourselves to be my disciples." (Jn 15:8)
8. Jesus, who said: "If you keep my commands, you will remain in my love, just as I have kept my Father's commands and remain in his love." (Jn 15:10)
9. Jesus, who said: "If they persecuted me, they will persecute you also. If they obeyed my teaching, they will obey yours also." (Jn 15:20)
10. Jesus, who said: "And you also must testify, for you have been with me from the beginning." (Jn 15:27)

## Decade V
1. Jesus, who said: "I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because you have hidden these things from the wise and learned, and revealed them to little children." (Mt 11:25)
2. Jesus, who said: "And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another advocate to help you and be with you forever—the Spirit of truth." (Jn 14:16–17a)
3. Jesus, who prayed: "I pray for them. I am not praying for the world, but for those you have given me, for they are yours." (Jn 17:9)
4. Jesus, who prayed: "Holy Father, protect them by the power of your name, the name you gave me, so that they may be one as we are one." (Jn 17:11)
5. Jesus, who prayed: "My prayer is not that you take them out of the world but that you protect them from the evil one." (Jn 17:15)
6. Jesus, who prayed: "Sanctify them by the truth; your word is truth." (Jn 17:17)
7. Jesus, who said: "For them I sanctify myself, that they too may be truly sanctified." (Jn 17:19)
8. Jesus, who said: "I have given them the glory that you gave me, that they may be one as we are one: I in them and you in me." (Jn 17:22–23a)
9. Jesus, who prayed: "Father, I want those you have given me to be with me where I am, and to see my glory." (Jn 17:24)
10. Jesus, who said to Simon: "Simon, Simon, Satan has asked to sift all of you as wheat. But I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith may not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers." (Lk 22:31–32)
