# The Holy Rosary (Posvátný růženec)

A Catholic guide, prayer companion, and interactive tracking tool for the devotion of the Most Holy Rosary:

- **Weekly & Daily Rosary Tracker:** Designed for **The Rosary Confraternity** (15 or 20 decades weekly) and daily rosary prayer according to Church guidance.
- **The Living Rosary (Živý růženec):** Group scheduler and link generator for 5-member prayer groups.
- **Scriptural Meditations:** Verse-by-verse meditations for all 20 mysteries of the Holy Rosary.

Published at: [https://vician.github.io/rosary/](https://vician.github.io/rosary/)
