title: Joyful Mysteries
---

_Source: Salesian Cooperators — Formation materials._

## I. The Annunciation
1. Whose coming was promised by God in paradise. (Gen 3:15)
2. Foretold by the prophets. (Isa 7:14)
3. To whom God promised the throne of David His father. (Lk 1:32)
4. Who will save His people from all their sins. (Mt 1:21)
5. Whose conception was announced to you by the angel. (Lk 1:28)
6. Of whom the angel said: "He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High." (Lk 1:32)
7. At whose conception the Holy Spirit came upon you. (Lk 1:35)
8. At whose conception the power of the Most High overshadowed you. (Lk 1:35)
9. At whose conception you said: "I am the Lord's servant." (Lk 1:38)
10. At whose conception you said: "May your word to me be fulfilled." (Lk 1:38)

## II. The Visitation
1. With whom you arose and went into the hill country with haste. (Lk 1:39)
2. With whom you entered the house of Zechariah and greeted Elizabeth. (Lk 1:40)
3. With whom you caused John to leap in his mother's womb. (Lk 1:41)
4. With whom you prayed: "My soul magnifies the Lord." (Lk 1:46)
5. With whom you prayed: "From now on all generations will call me blessed." (Lk 1:48)
6. With whom you prayed: "The Mighty One has done great things for me." (Lk 1:49)
7. With whom you prayed: "He has brought down rulers from their thrones." (Lk 1:52)
8. With whom you prayed: "He has filled the hungry with good things." (Lk 1:53)
9. With whom you stayed with Elizabeth for about three months. (Lk 1:56)
10. For whom John prepared the way. (Lk 1:76)

## III. The Nativity
1. With whom you went up from Nazareth to Bethlehem. (Lk 2:4)
2. For whom there was no room in the inn. (Lk 2:7)
3. Whom you brought forth in Bethlehem of Judea. (Mt 2:1)
4. Whom you wrapped in swaddling cloths and laid in a manger. (Lk 2:7)
5. Who was your firstborn Son. (Lk 2:7)
6. At whose birth the heavenly hosts praised God. (Lk 2:13)
7. Whom the shepherds hurried to see and worship. (Lk 2:16)
8. To whom the Magi from the East brought gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh. (Mt 2:11)
9. Whom Herod sought to destroy. (Mt 2:13)
10. Whom you and Joseph saved by fleeing into Egypt. (Mt 2:14)

## IV. The Presentation in the Temple
1. Who was circumcised on the eighth day. (Lk 2:21)
2. Whom you presented to the Lord in the temple at Jerusalem. (Lk 2:22)
3. For whom the sacrifice was offered according to the Law of the Lord. (Lk 2:24)
4. Whom righteous Simeon took in his arms and blessed God. (Lk 2:28)
5. Who is the salvation prepared in the presence of all peoples. (Lk 2:30)
6. Who is a light for revelation to the Gentiles. (Lk 2:32)
7. Who is a sign that will be spoken against. (Lk 2:34)
8. Who is appointed for the fall and rising of many. (Lk 2:34)
9. Of whom the prophetess Anna spoke to all who were awaiting redemption. (Lk 2:38)
10. With whom you returned to Nazareth in Galilee. (Lk 2:39)

## V. The Finding in the Temple
1. Who grew and became strong, filled with wisdom. (Lk 2:40)
2. Whom you took to Jerusalem for the Feast of the Passover at twelve years of age. (Lk 2:42)
3. Who stayed behind in Jerusalem without His parents knowing. (Lk 2:43)
4. Whom you and Joseph searched for among relatives and acquaintances. (Lk 2:44)
5. Whom after three days you found in the temple courts. (Lk 2:46)
6. Who was sitting among the teachers, listening and asking questions. (Lk 2:46)
7. At whose understanding and answers all were amazed. (Lk 2:47)
8. To whom you said: "Son, why have you treated us like this?" (Lk 2:48)
9. Who said: "Why were you searching for me? Did you not know I had to be in my Father's house?" (Lk 2:49)
10. Who went down to Nazareth with you and was obedient to you. (Lk 2:51)
